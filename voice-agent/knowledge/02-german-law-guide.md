# Everyday German Law and Bureaucracy — General Information Guide

IMPORTANT: This is general information, not legal advice (Rechtsberatung). Amounts and rules change often.
For a specific case, people should go to a lawyer (Rechtsanwalt), a free counselling office (Beratungsstelle),
the Verbraucherzentrale, a Mieterverein, a trade union, or the Migrationsberatung.

## Free or cheap legal help
- Beratungshilfe: people with low income can get a voucher (Beratungsschein) at the local Amtsgericht
  for advice from a lawyer. The lawyer may charge only a small fee (about 15 euros).
- Prozesskostenhilfe (PKH): state help to pay court costs for people with low income, applied for at the court.
- Migrationsberatung für erwachsene Zuwanderer (MBE) and Jugendmigrationsdienst: free help for migrants
  with letters, applications and residence questions. Run by Caritas, Diakonie, AWO, DRK and others.
- Verbraucherzentrale: consumer problems (contracts, online shopping, energy bills).
- Mieterverein: tenant problems (rent, Nebenkosten, deposits). Membership costs a small yearly fee.
- Schuldnerberatung: free debt counselling (Caritas, Diakonie, city).

## Most important deadlines (Fristen)
Deadlines usually start on the day the letter is delivered (Zustellung / Bekanntgabe).
A letter sent by post normally counts as delivered on the 4th day after it was sent (since 2025; before it was the 3rd day).
- Widerspruch against a decision of an authority (Jobcenter, Wohngeldstelle, Familienkasse, etc.): 1 month.
  If the letter has no correct Rechtsbehelfsbelehrung, the deadline is 1 year.
- Einspruch against a tax decision (Steuerbescheid): 1 month.
- Einspruch against a fine (Bußgeldbescheid): 2 weeks.
- Einspruch against a Strafbefehl: 2 weeks.
- Widerspruch against a Mahnbescheid (court payment order): 2 weeks.
- Kündigungsschutzklage after being fired from a job: 3 weeks at the Arbeitsgericht.
- Widerrufsrecht for online/phone purchases: 14 days.
- Statutory warranty (Gewährleistung) for new goods: 2 years.
- Regular limitation period (Verjährung) for most claims: 3 years, starting at the end of the year.
Advice: always keep a copy, send important letters by Einschreiben or fax with confirmation, or hand them in and get a stamp.

## Social benefits (Sozialleistungen)
- Bürgergeld / Grundsicherung für Arbeitsuchende (Jobcenter, SGB II): basic income for people who can work
  but do not earn enough. The system is being reformed into a "Neue Grundsicherung"; check current rules.
- Wohngeld (Wohngeldstelle of the city): rent subsidy for people with low income who do not get Bürgergeld.
- Kindergeld (Familienkasse): money for each child, usually until 18, longer if in training/studies (up to 25).
- Kinderzuschlag (Familienkasse): extra money for families with low income, together with Kindergeld.
  Often combined with Wohngeld.
- Elterngeld (Elterngeldstelle): after the birth of a child, apply within the first 3 months for full payment.
- Bildung und Teilhabe (BuT): school trips, school supplies, lunch, tutoring for children of low-income families.
- Grundsicherung im Alter (Sozialamt): for older people or people unable to work with too little pension.
Tip: if a benefit is refused, file a Widerspruch within 1 month. It is free.

## Residence and migration (Aufenthaltsrecht)
- Responsible office: Ausländerbehörde of the city or district.
- Apply to extend a residence permit BEFORE it expires. If you apply in time, you get a Fiktionsbescheinigung
  and stay legal while they decide.
- Naturalisation (Einbürgerung): since June 2024 usually possible after 5 years of legal residence
  (3 years with special integration achievements, rules may change), with German B1, secure livelihood,
  Einbürgerungstest. Dual citizenship is generally allowed since June 2024.
- Family reunification, work permits, Blue Card: depend on the case; check with Ausländerbehörde or Migrationsberatung.

## Work (Arbeitsrecht)
- Minimum wage applies to almost all employees (amount changes yearly).
- Notice period (Kündigungsfrist) for employees: basic 4 weeks to the 15th or end of a month; longer for the employer
  if you have worked there for many years.
- Termination must be in writing on paper with signature. Email or WhatsApp is not valid.
- After termination: register with the Agentur für Arbeit within 3 days of learning about it (arbeitsuchend melden).

## Renting (Mietrecht)
- A tenant can end a normal rental contract with 3 months' notice, in writing.
- Deposit (Kaution): maximum 3 months' cold rent; may be paid in 3 instalments.
- Nebenkostenabrechnung: must arrive within 12 months after the billing period ends; tenant may check receipts.
- Rent increase: usually needs the tenant's consent and has limits (Kappungsgrenze, Mietpreisbremse in many cities).

## Writing to an authority (Behörde)
- Always write your name, address, file number (Aktenzeichen / Kundennummer / BG-Nummer), date and signature.
- Short and clear: what you want, why, and attachments.
- Keep proof of sending.
